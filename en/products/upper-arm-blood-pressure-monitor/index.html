In plain words

Put the cuff on your upper arm, press the button, and after about 40 seconds the screen shows your two blood pressure numbers and your pulse. It keeps the last 60 readings, so your doctor can see how your blood pressure has moved over the week.

When you need it

  • Daily checks for high blood pressure
  • Follow-up after a change of medication
  • When you feel dizzy or have a headache

How to use it

  1. 1Sit quietly for 5 minutes with your back supported.
  2. 2Place the cuff 2 cm above the elbow, at heart level.
  3. 3Press the button and don’t talk during the reading.
  4. 4Measure twice, a minute apart, and note both.

Nurse’s tip

Measure at the same time each day, before breakfast and before your medication.
