How it works

  1. 1

    Get in touch

    Call us, message us on WhatsApp or by email, on our social media (Instagram, Facebook), or leave your number in the form and we’ll contact you.

  2. 2

    The doctor comes home

    They get to know the patient and learn about their medical history, including ongoing conditions, any past surgery and current treatment. Then they carry out a physical examination and give their recommendations.

  3. 3

    Choose the plan

    A monthly package or only the services you need. The doctor advises, you decide.

  4. 4

    Your online chart

    Every visit is recorded in the online medical chart. You also get the report on WhatsApp or by email.

  1. 1

    The doctor comes for a first visit

    They get to know the patient and their medical history, then measure the vital signs: blood pressure, pulse, oxygen saturation and a random blood sugar.

  2. 2

    Everything goes into the online chart

    During the visit the doctor records in the app the vital signs, the symptoms the patient reports, the physical examination, the treatment, and every lab test and imaging study done before. Nothing stays only on paper.

  3. 3

    Choose how to continue

    Become a member with one of the monthly packages, or book services only when you need them. Every service is recorded in the chart either way.

  4. 4

    Members open their own chart

    With a monthly or yearly package, you sign in to the app and see your whole medical history: visits, lab results with graphs of how they change, and imaging studies, all documented and stored by the date they were done.

  5. 5

    Explanations and advice from the doctor

    The doctor explains your test results in plain words, notes your current health status and the recommendations going forward. You see them as soon as they are added.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions we hear most.

Didn’t find your answer?

Call or message us. A nurse answers, not a robot.

CallWhatsApp
Where do you visit?

We come to your home, wherever you are. Ambulance transport outside the city is priced by distance. Call us and we will tell you straight away when we can come.

Who are the professionals that visit?

Our team includes general practitioners and specialists, nurses, physiotherapists, psychologists and social workers. Who visits depends on the patient’s needs and the package you choose.

What does the medical report include?

Vital signs (blood pressure, pulse, oxygen saturation, blood sugar, temperature, respiratory rate), symptoms, the examination, the treatment given and recommendations. Reports are kept by date and sent to you by email or WhatsApp.

How does the online medical chart work?

On the first visit the doctor opens your chart in the app and records your vital signs, history, examination and treatment. Every visit, test, ultrasound or X-ray is added by date. On a monthly package you open the chart yourself and see your history, current status, explanations of your test results and the doctor’s recommendations.

Who can see my medical data?

Only you and the professionals caring for you, each according to their role. The service’s administrators never see personal health data.

Can I book a single service without a package?

Yes. Injections, dressings, blood draws, home ultrasound, catheter care, physiotherapy and our other services can be booked one at a time. Every service is recorded in the medical report.

How much do the packages cost?

The price depends on the address, the patient’s condition and the package. After a short call we give you an exact price, with no obligation.

What do you offer businesses?

Occupational medicine: doctor and nurse visits at your office, clinical records on paper and online at no extra cost, and on the Premium package monthly first aid training for your staff.

What should I do in an emergency?

In an emergency call 127 straight away. Clients on the Medium and Complete packages also have 24/7 medical phone support.
